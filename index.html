About

Aerospace engineering student at the University of New South Wales, with hands-on experience spanning computational fluid dynamics, structural analysis, aircraft conceptual design and rapid prototyping. Comfortable moving between simulation, hand calculation and the workshop floor to take a design from concept through to a physical, tested part.

Particularly interested in high-speed aerodynamics and propulsion, developed through thesis work on scramjet inlet flow, alongside a broad base of applied mechanical and structural design experience from team and personal projects.

Projects

A selection of thesis, team and personal engineering work. Each project can hold several photos — use the arrows or dots to browse.

Undergraduate thesis

CFD & Experimental Investigation of Scramjet Inlet Flow and Fuel Mixing

Investigated scramjet inlet aerodynamics and fuel–air mixing behaviour through a combination of CFD simulation and experimental wind-tunnel testing, characterising inlet flow structures and evaluating mixing performance under hypersonic-relevant conditions.

Team project

General Aviation Aircraft Conceptual Design

Worked within a multidisciplinary team to design a general aviation aircraft against a defined specification, from performance sizing and aerodynamic layout through to a complete conceptual design of the airframe.

Team design project

Motor-Actuated Passcode Safe

Designed the external enclosure and contributed to the development and integration of the electromechanical locking system, coordinating the mechanical housing with the motor-driven locking mechanism and electrical control.

Collaborative project

Wind Energy & Cyclone Risk Monitoring

Collaborated on the design of a concept combining small-scale wind energy harvesting with real-time wind and weather monitoring, aimed at improving resilience and early warning in cyclone-prone regions.

Individual project

Helicopter Winch Lug — Structural Analysis & Manufacturing

Designed and manufactured a helicopter winch lug from aluminium, bolts and rivets. Carried out independent structural calculations and analysed the lug under limit and ultimate loading conditions to assess its strength and safety margins.

Personal project

Lightweight Chassis — Uphill Traction Optimisation

Designed and 3D-printed a lightweight chassis optimised for traction performance on uphill terrain, balancing structural weight against grip and load distribution.

Personal project

Modular Wall-Mounted Hat Rack

Designed and 3D-printed a modular hat rack system with interlocking wall connections, allowing the storage layout to be reconfigured to suit different spaces.
